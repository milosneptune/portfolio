It seems you want to learn about me, ask and I shall tell!

I've always been fascinated by animation. Since high school, I've been doing freelance illustrations and character design. In my later years, I picked up programming as a hobby and joined FIRST Robotics. I was captivated by the software development, and thus I realized I wanted to work in a field that combined visuals, code, and interactivity. 

Game development continues to inspire me and its multidisciplinary aspect proves how powerful the game medium can be. I want to develop and design impactful games that make use of its unique medium. 

As a woman of color, I commit to uplifting others in this field. I understand firsthand the importance of representation, and I seek to help inspire the next generation in a way that wasn't done for me. 

At Rochester Institute of Technology, I'm furthering my knowledge in object-oriented programming languages, game engines, web development, and experience design. At the moment, I am seeking opportunities for interdisciplinary research about culture, people, and games. I am also fascinated by UI/UX design, game design and sociology, critical game studies, and more.

My previous work experience can be found on my resume. I've worked as an Experience Designer & Researcher at Honda for their in vehicle infotainment systems, where I produced 2D and 3D graphics and models, Figma prototypes, and conducted research studies.

Past my work, I  enjoy arts and crafts, especially textile arts! You can find me almost always carrying my hook and yarn everywhere with me, waiting for the opportunity to strike.
